Privacy Policy

Jargon does not collect any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not send your data anywhere — because it does not send anything anywhere.

Last updated: 14 October 2026. This policy covers the Jargon mobile app and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. Jargon has no sign-up and no login, so there is no account or profile associated with you.

This website sets no cookies and runs no scripts, analytics or trackers.

What the app stores on your device

To do its job, Jargon saves your study data in a private database on your phone:

  • which words you have studied and how you answered them
  • your review schedule, mastery buckets, streak and daily goal
  • your exam date, if you enter one
  • your app settings

This never leaves your device. We cannot see it and we have no way to retrieve it. It lives in the app's own private storage, which other apps on your phone cannot read.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Jargon contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. We use no device identifiers and work with no ad networks or data brokers.

Purchases

GRE mode is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or receive your name, card number or billing address — only a confirmation from the store that a purchase was made, so the app can unlock the content you paid for. Apple and Google handle your payment information under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Jargon does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Children

Jargon is a study tool for graduate school entrance exams and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Jargon removes its database from your device and permanently deletes everything it saved. Because none of it was ever sent to us, there is nothing on our side to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will post the updated version here and revise the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to support@mcgrinderlabs.com.
