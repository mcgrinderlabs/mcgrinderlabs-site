Support

Email support@mcgrinderlabs.com and you'll hear back within 3 business days, usually sooner.

It helps to include your device (iPhone or Android), the app version from Settings, and a screenshot if something looks wrong.

Restore your GRE mode purchase

GRE mode is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple or Google account, so you never pay twice. On a new phone, or after reinstalling:

  1. Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.
  2. Open Jargon and go to Settings.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

If GRE mode still doesn't unlock, email us with the date you bought it. Don't send card details; we never need them.

Common questions

Is Jargon a subscription?

No. The app is free with 145 essential words and every feature. GRE mode is a single $9.99 purchase that you keep.

Does my iPhone purchase carry over to Android, or the other way round?

No. Apple and Google handle purchases separately, so a purchase restores only on the same platform it was made on.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google process refunds for app purchases. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the Google Play order history. Email us if you get stuck.

Will I lose my progress if I switch phones?

Email us before you switch and we'll tell you what carries over for your version of the app.

I found a wrong definition or a typo.

Please tell us. Send the word and what's wrong to support@mcgrinderlabs.com.
